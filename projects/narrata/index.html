Narrata - Your ebooks narrated

Read your EPUB and PDF files or listen through text-to-speech in a private local library designed to work offline.

Android Text-to-Speech EPUB PDF
View demo

Narrata is a modern e-book and audiobook reader designed for comfortable, distraction-free reading and listening. It brings EPUB and PDF files, text-to-speech narration, and a personal library together in one focused experience.

Features

  • EPUB and PDF reading: Import your own books and keep them in an organized library
  • Text-to-speech narration: Listen to the text of your e-books with high-quality narration
  • Audiobook support: Keep reading and listening together in a single application
  • Remembered progress: Pick up each book exactly where you left off
  • Local library: Your imported content remains on your device
  • Offline access: Keep reading and listening after your books have been added

Reading Experience

The clean interface keeps the focus on the content and minimizes distractions. Narrata makes it easy to move between reading and listening during commutes, workouts, or whenever you want to rest your eyes.

Privacy

The library and imported files are managed locally on the device. Narrata does not share data with third parties.
