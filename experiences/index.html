About this section

I build digital products across web, mobile, and automation contexts, with a strong focus on full-stack JavaScript and TypeScript ecosystems. My experience spans product delivery, interface design, backend architecture, and workflow tooling.

Over the years, I have contributed to SaaS platforms, internal business tools, data-driven systems, and client-facing applications. That variety helped me grow as an engineer who can move from strategy and UX thinking to implementation details and long-term maintainability.

In 2024, I moved to Montreal for an international experience as a Full Stack Developer at Acensi. It added a new cultural and professional perspective to my work, after graduating from Epitech with a Master of Science in 2022 and building a foundation in AI, big data, and modern software development.
